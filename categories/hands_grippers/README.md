## Comparison

This is the largest category in the first batch. Low-cost 3D-printed tendon or hybrid hands (LEAP Hand v2 at a stated ~USD 200 class, with an official August 2025 parts split of USD 221.95 Feetech + USD 251.53 other; Robot Nano Hand; DexHand) sit beside Dynamixel-based research hands (LEAP Hand v1, RUKA, ORCA, LEAP Hand v2 Advanced). Licenses are not uniform: several LEAP releases use non-commercial Creative Commons terms on CAD or even on the v1 API, while ORCA hardware is CC BY 4.0 (including commercial use with attribution) and RUKA's repo is MIT. eFlesh and FreeTacMan are tactile sensing systems rather than complete hands; they are kept here because they are usually used with dexterous manipulation, with their type marked in the data file. Dextra is a much older Hackaday-era design; several fields remain Unknown.
