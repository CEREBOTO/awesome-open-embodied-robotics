## Comparison

Cost and arm choice dominate the differences. XLeRobot is explicitly a dual SO-101 plus omni-wheel home robot; the official README gives a basic BOM around USD 660 (excluding printing, tools, shipping, and tax) and sits in the LeRobot ecosystem. AhaRobot is a low-cost bimanual mobile manipulator from Tianjin University, with Feetech actuation and GPL-3.0 hardware and software repositories; an official single-number BOM was not confirmed in the pages we opened. MABEL is a 56-DOF whole-body platform (OpenArm-derived arms and ORCA-derived hands); official Essential, Recommended, and Maximum listed parts subtotals are about US$8,056, US$9,283, and US$14,721 (July 2026 price basis). TidyBot++ is a holonomic swerve-drive research mobile base (Kraken X60 / CAN) designed to carry interchangeable arms (Kinova, Franka, or ARX5 in the official BOM). The official Kinova-tab mobile-base total is USD 5,379.63, not including the arm itself, so a complete system price depends on which arm you mount.
